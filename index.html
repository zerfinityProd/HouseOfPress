Design Philosophy

The Art of Deep Impressions.

In an era dominated by transient digital pixels, physical tactile presence is the ultimate mark of luxury. We eliminate visual noise to let texture, light, and depth speak for your brand.

01

Uncompromising Slowness

True letterpress printing cannot be automated or rushed. Each cotton sheet is hand-fed, pressure-calibrated, and individually inspected under natural daylight in our studio.

02

Archival 600–900 GSM Cotton

We print exclusively on tree-free, 100% cotton paper sourced from historic European mills. Its plush texture allows deep blind deboss impressions without show-through on the reverse side.

03

Hand-Mixed Inks & Foil Alloys

From rich crimson oil inks mixed by eye to hot stamp brass foils in warm gold, rose bronze, and deep metallic copper, our palette is tuned for architectural elegance.

Methodology

The Five-Stage Craft Process

01

Typography & Vector Proofing

We analyze your artwork for deboss suitability. Lines are vector-adjusted to ensure optimal depth without tearing the paper fibers during pressing.
02

Photopolymer & Zinc Plate Molding

High-precision magnesium or zinc plates are etched from your approved proof, creating a razor-sharp 3D die that will bite deep into the cotton stock.
03

Hand Inking & Color Matching

Inks are mixed manually on glass slabs using oil-based Pantone formulas to achieve ultra-rich opacity and velvety tactile matte finishes.
04

Deep Impression Cast-Iron Pressing

Using thousands of pounds of pressure on vintage platen presses, each card receives a deep, tactile bite that catches ambient room light.
05

Edge Painting & Archival Packaging

Edges are hand-gilded with gold foil or hand-painted in signature crimson ink, then packed in velvet-lined gift boxes.
